Good to Know

Common Questions

How much does excavation work cost?

Every site is different — access, soil, ledge, and how much material has to move all factor in. That's why estimates are free: we look at the actual job and give you a real number, not a guess over the phone.

Do I need to call Dig Safe first?

We handle that. Utility mark-out gets scheduled before any digging starts — it's the law, and it's how everyone goes home safe.

How big a job will you take on?

From a weekend-sized drainage fix to a full pool dig, demolition, or site prep for new construction. If it involves moving dirt, it's worth a call — if it's not a fit, we'll tell you straight.

Do you work in the winter?

Yes — frost and snow don't shut us down. Foundation digs, egress windows, and emergency drainage work happen year-round.

What about my lawn and landscaping?

We plan machine access before we start and keep the footprint as tight as the job allows. When we're done, the site gets graded and cleaned up — not left looking like a battlefield.
